Screenshots Intake Routing Rulebook
Date: 2026-06-06

Scope
- Intake and routing only.
- No file moves executed from this workspace.
- No seeding action should be triggered from this document.

Required routing buckets
1) Food truck / restaurant / caterer / concession
- Destination project: Merlin evidence seed
- Destination folder: MealScout_Merlin_Evidence_Seeds
- Seed action: seed_to_merlin_evidence

2) Contractors and home services (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping)
- Destination project: TradeScout contractor intake
- Destination folder: TradeScout_Contractors by trade
- Seed action: do_not_seed_to_mealscout

3) Food-related but ambiguous
- Destination project: Merlin review or quarantine
- Destination folder: Restaurants_Needs_Review
- Seed action: hold_for_review_before_seed

4) Clearly unrelated, social, personal, low-value, or duplicate
- Destination project: Archive or review
- Destination folder: Duplicate_or_Low_Quality
- Seed action: archive_or_review

5) Unknown project fit
- Destination project: Unknown project triage
- Destination folder: _Needs_Review
- Seed action: hold_for_manual_classification

Accepted safety posture to preserve for any food screenshot sent to Merlin evidence seed
- profile_origin = evidence_seed
- seeded_from_evidence = true
- claim_status = unclaimed
- email_verified = false
- insurance_verified = false
- owner_user_id = null

Loose or admin-unattributed affiliate posture
- affiliate_attribution = null
- affiliate_email = null
- affiliate_source_folder = null
- attribution_method = admin_unattributed

Batch operation sequence
1) Route food screenshots to Merlin evidence seed input.
2) Route contractor screenshots to TradeScout contractor folders.
3) Route ambiguous screenshots to review folders.
4) Route duplicates and low-quality screenshots to archive or review.

Manifest source file
- screenshots-routing-manifest-initial.csv
