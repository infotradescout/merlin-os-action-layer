## Endpoints Added
1. `POST /api/mealscout/intake/batches/:batchId/quality/score`
- role-gated to admin/operator/staff
- recomputes quality for batch + all batch cards
- persists card quality fields
- persists batch quality aggregate
- returns advisory quality result envelope
- `mutationAllowed:false`

2. `GET /api/mealscout/intake/affiliates/quality-summary`
- role-gated to admin/operator/staff
- returns affiliate-level quality aggregates:
  - high/medium/low/blocked counts
  - averageQualityScore
  - applied/open/claim counts
  - qualityWarnings
- `mutationAllowed:false`
- no payout math

3. `PATCH /api/mealscout/intake/action-cards/:cardId/quality/override` (optional preferred in spec)
- role-gated
- requires `qualityReason`
- manual band override with audit signal/warning capture
- does not mutate apply/payout/notification/profile states
- `mutationAllowed:false`

## Scoring Behavior
Added deterministic card scoring with:

Positive signals (examples):
- identity_name_present
- contact_signal_present
- location_signal_present
- menu_or_service_details_present
- multi_file_coherent_cluster
- high_confidence_card
- applied_profile_change
- profile_link_generated
- profile_link_opened
- claim_or_review_started

Negative/warning signals (examples):
- duplicate_conflict_unresolved
- conflict_warning_unresolved
- replacement_candidate_unresolved
- card_rejected
- apply_failed_safety
- missing_required_identity_fields
- low_extraction_confidence
- no_valid_recipient
- no_profile_link
- insufficient_evidence

Banding:
- `blocked` for rejected/apply-failed/replacement-risk safety conditions
- `high` for strong score with no unresolved conflict warnings
- `medium` for usable but incomplete/conflicted states
- `low` for weak/unsafe evidence
- `unscored` when insufficient processed evidence exists
