- update_menu biases menu classification confidence.
- update_schedule biases schedule classification confidence.
- add_logo biases logo classification confidence.
- add_photos biases photo classification confidence.
- raw_dump runs general evidence-based classification.

- Preview endpoint returns draft-only changes and warning text; no publish path invoked.
- Non-MealScout brand routes are explicitly held in v1 (no cross-brand routing).
